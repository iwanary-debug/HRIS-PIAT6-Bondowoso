# HRIS PIAT6 — Roadmap

## Phase 1 — Foundation
Auth, shell, roles/permissions, dashboard, employee master, organization,
position/unit, search/filter, employee detail.

## Phase 2 — Import & Core HR
XLSX/CSV import, mapping, validation, duplicate handling, contract/SK,
employment history, documents.

## Phase 3 — Daily HR
Attendance, leave/permission, approval, notifications, dashboard indicators.

## Phase 4 — Compensation & Benefits
Payroll foundation, salary components, BPJS, benefit, payroll reports.
Do not invent local payroll rules that are not specified; make configurable.

## Phase 5 — Development
Education, competency, training, performance, discipline, coaching,
promotion/mutation.

## Phase 6 — Reports & Governance
Reports, export, audit, data quality, backup/recovery readiness.

## Phase 7 — Integration
Prepare/adapt the HRIS domain module for PIAT 6 Super App.
Do not duplicate the employee master when integration is performed.
